Important Notes:
----------------
1. GPU Requirements:
   - TRELLIS.2 requires an NVIDIA GPU with at least 24GB VRAM
   - Ensure your cluster has GPU nodes with the NVIDIA device plugin installed

2. Model Download:
   - The TRELLIS.2-4B model (~15GB) downloads automatically on first run
   - This may take several minutes depending on your connection
   - Model is cached in the persistent volume for subsequent runs

3. Startup Time:
   - Initial startup may take 2-5 minutes for model loading
   - The startup probe is configured with a generous timeout

{{- if not .Values.persistence.modelCache.enabled }}

         Enable persistence.modelCache.enabled for production use.
{{- end }}
